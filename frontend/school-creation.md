The platform allows **users or organizations to create schools** (essentially *mini academies* or *teaching organizations* under your platform), we want a **tiered verification system** that ensures legitimacy, accountability, and smooth management of tutors and students under those schools.

Let’s break this down clearly and logically:

---

## 🏫 What “Create a School” Means

A **school** on your platform might:

* Contain multiple **tutors** (internal staff or contractors)
* Offer **courses** under its name
* Handle its own **students**, **billing**, and **certification**
* Possibly have a **custom dashboard** (logo, branding, analytics)

So before you approve a school, you must confirm that:

1. It’s a legitimate entity (not a scam)
2. It has qualified tutors or staff
3. It can legally and ethically teach under your terms

---

## 🧾 1. Basic School Information

**Purpose:** Establish the organization’s identity.

Ask for:

* **Official school name**
* **Legal business name** (if registered)
* **Business registration number**
* **Country of registration**
* **Business type** (sole proprietor, partnership, LLC, NGO, etc.)
* **Primary contact person** (name, email, phone)
* **School website or social media links**
* **School logo**
* **Short description** (mission, specialization, subjects offered)

---

## 🪪 2. Verification Documents

**Purpose:** Ensure authenticity and protect against fraud.

Request uploads of:

* **Business registration certificate**
* **Tax identification / PIN certificate**
* **Proof of address / physical location** (utility bill, lease, etc.)
* **Identity document of founder / admin** (national ID, passport)
* **Letter of authorization** (if the person registering isn’t the founder)

Optional but valuable:

* **Accreditation documents** (if applicable, e.g., Ministry of Education, TESOL Board)
* **Insurance certificate** (if they handle in-person or liability-sensitive activities)

---

## 👩🏫 3. Staff and Tutor Details

**Purpose:** Validate teaching quality under the school.

Ask the school admin to provide:

* **List of tutors / instructors** (names, emails, roles)
* Each tutor’s **verification status** (must be individually verified or pending)
* **Admin/staff roles** (who manages students, billing, etc.)
* **Contract terms** (whether tutors are internal or freelance)

Optional:

* Ability for schools to **invite** tutors already verified on the platform
* Option to **submit tutor verification requests** on behalf of new hires

---

## 💳 4. Financial Information

**Purpose:** Handle payouts, tuition, and revenue share properly.

Ask for:

* **Preferred payout method** (Stripe, Wise, PayPal, local bank)
* **Business bank account details** (matching registered name)
* **Currency preference**
* **Revenue model selection:**

  * Commission-based (percentage to platform)
  * Subscription-based (fixed monthly fee)
  * Hybrid model

Optional:

* **Tax compliance documents** (especially if payouts exceed legal thresholds)
* **Bank statement** (for verification)

---

## 🧠 5. Educational Scope and Curriculum

**Purpose:** Ensure subjects and content are legitimate and align with your policies.

Ask for:

* **Subjects / categories offered**
* **Target learners** (age groups, proficiency levels, corporate vs. individual)
* **Teaching format** (live classes, recorded, hybrid, mentorship)
* **Languages of instruction**
* **Curriculum outline or course catalog**
* **Sample course material or syllabus**
* **Demo video / promotional video** (for marketing and review)

---

## 🔐 6. Policies and Legal Agreements

**Purpose:** Ensure legal compliance and protect your platform.

Require acceptance of:

* ✅ **School Partnership Agreement**
* ✅ **Data Protection & Privacy Policy**
* ✅ **Revenue Split / Commission Terms**
* ✅ **Code of Conduct for Tutors and Admins**
* ✅ **Quality Assurance Policy**
* ✅ **Anti-Plagiarism & Anti-Fraud Declaration**
* ✅ **Student Refund Policy**

Optional:

* **Liability waiver**
* **Content ownership terms**
* **Advertising consent** (allowing you to feature their school publicly)

---

## 🧾 7. Optional but Powerful Additions

| Category                   | Description                                                       |
| -------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Marketing Materials**    | Upload promotional images, videos, or flyers                      |
| **Testimonials / Reviews** | Collect from students for credibility                             |
| **Brand Customization**    | Custom banner, subdomain, and color theme                         |
| **Integrations**           | Option to connect to their LMS or CRM (e.g., Moodle, HubSpot)     |
| **Partnership Type**       | Select “Independent”, “Corporate Training”, or “Community School” |

---

## ✅ 8. Approval Workflow

1. **Application Submission**

   * School fills out form + uploads documents
2. **Platform Review**

   * Staff verifies documents and legitimacy
3. **Optional video call** (short 10-min interview with school admin)
4. **Status:**

   * ✅ Verified School (green badge)
   * 🕓 Pending Verification
   * ❌ Rejected (with feedback)

Once approved, the school can:

* Add or invite tutors
* Publish courses under its name
* Manage students and payments
